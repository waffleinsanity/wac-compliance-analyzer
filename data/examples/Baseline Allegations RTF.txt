# Baseline allegation language — subsection-citation style
# Used as preferred templates when drafting Investigative Report allegations.

Allegation: Potential violation of WAC 246-337-045, Governance and administration, by having failed to (1)(a)(iii) adopting, periodically reviewing, and updating as necessary, policies that govern a policy addressing the provision of sufficient resources such as personnel, facilities, equipment, and supplies to meet the needs of the population served; (1)(b) provide a process for communication and conflict resolution for both staff and residents; (3)(b) staff are assigned, oriented, trained, supervised, monitored, and evaluated; and (3)(c) staff who provide direct resident care, direct treatment, or manage the safety of a resident are competent by training, experience and capability.

Allegation: Potential violation of WAC 246-337-048, Quality improvement program, by having failed to (2)(b) collect, measure, and assess data on allegations of abuse; (2)(e) collect, measure, and assess data on injuries which result in serious or unanticipated outcomes; (2)(g) collect, measure, and assess data on resident grievances; (2)(h) collect, measure, and assess data on security incidents; and (4) implement and document changes or improvements made to prevent future occurrences of any serious or unanticipated resident outcome specified in subsection (2) of this section.

Allegation: Potential violation of WAC 246-337-075, Resident rights, by having failed to (2)(a) be free of abuse, including being deprived of food, clothes, or other basic necessities; (2)(e) review or have their personal representative, designee, or parent or guardian review the resident's files in accordance with chapter 70.02 RCW; and (6) account for each resident's assets, including allowance, earnings from federal or state sources and expenditures.

Allegation: Potential violation of WAC 246-337-080, Resident care services, by having failed to (2)(c) refer residents for health care provided outside of the RTF as needed such as, but not limited to, laboratory, dental, ambulatory care or specialty services as needed; (2)(f) provide access to a health assessment by a health care prescriber any time a resident exhibits signs or symptoms of an injury, illness or abnormality for which a medical diagnosis and treatment are indicated; and (2)(j) assure provisions of each resident's personal care items and durable medical equipment including storing and labeling each resident's personal care items separately, preventing contamination, and preventing access by other residents.

Allegation: Potential violation of WAC 246-337-085, Accepting a child with a parent in treatment, by having failed to (5) the RTF shall obtain a health history for each child following admission and, if needed, develop with the parent a plan of care for each child that addresses the child's health care needs, including medications.

Allegation: Potential violation of WAC 246-337-100, Resident's individual service plan, by having failed to (2)(b) address the needs of a mother and baby during pregnancy and after delivery, if applicable.
